Privacy

Privacy notice

This site collects as little as it can. Here is what is kept, why, and how to ask for it to be removed.

Who runs this site

poddle.ie is a personal, non-commercial project run by Mark Finegan in Dublin. You can contact me through the form on the About page.

Visitor counts

The site counts visits with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and does not track you across sites. It records which page was viewed, the site you came from, your browser and screen size and your country. Your IP address is not stored. There is no advertising on this site.

Messages you send

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are passed through FormSubmit and delivered to me by email. I use them only to reply to you, and delete them when they are no longer needed, usually within a year.

Buying a workbook

Workbooks are sold through Payhip, which handles payment and delivery under its own privacy policy. Payhip shares your name, email address and what you bought with me so I can help with any problem with your order. I never see your card details.

Schools

I may email a school’s published office address to tell it about this free resource. I keep the school’s name, its published email address and the principal’s name, because it is in my legitimate interest to tell local schools about a free local-history resource, and the impact on them is small. Each email says how to opt out, and if a school asks me not to write again I keep a note of that so I never do. I delete the other details after a year if there is no reply.

Other sites

The maps load street tiles from OpenStreetMap and the text uses Google Fonts, so your browser connects to those services to fetch them. Links to Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything I hold about you, or object to my using it, through the contact form. If you are unhappy with how I have handled your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission.
