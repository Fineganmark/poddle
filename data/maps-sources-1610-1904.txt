HISTORIC DUBLIN MAPS - SOURCES (compiled 5 Nov 2026)
====================================================

1) speed-1610.jpg
-----------------
Title:       "Dubline" - inset plan of Dublin from John Speed's map of Leinster
             (The Theatre of the Empire of Great Britaine). Caption on this
             copy: "From JOHN SPEED'S ORIGINAL MAP, SURVEYED IN 1610."
Date:        Surveyed 1610 (published 1611/12). This scan is of a 19th-century
             reprint in "Remains of St. Mary's Abbey, Dublin. Their
             explorations and researches, A.D. 1886" (Dublin: Forster & Co.,
             1887), page 10. Commons calls it the "reprint of 1896".
Maker:       John Speed (1551/2-1629). Scan by the British Library
             (shelfmarks HMNTS 10390.h.14 / 4735.g.4), via Flickr Commons.
Downloaded:  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Dublin_in_1610_-_reprint_of_1896.jpg?width=2400
             (redirected to the full original:
             https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/50/Dublin_in_1610_-_reprint_of_1896.jpg)
File page:   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dublin_in_1610_-_reprint_of_1896.jpg
Wikipedia:   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Speed%27s_Map_of_Dublin_(1610)
Licence:     Public domain ({{PD-old}} on Commons). British Library Flickr
             Commons release, "no known copyright restrictions".
Pixel size:  2676 x 2288 (the full original. Commons didn't serve a smaller
             2400 px copy, so this is the highest-resolution version.)
Credit line: John Speed, "Dubline", 1610. British Library / Wikimedia Commons (public domain).

2) strangways-1904.jpg
----------------------
Title:       Map of the walls of Dublin (also catalogued as "Leonard
             Strangeway's map of Medieval Dublin City" by the RSAI). Commons
             quotes the map as: "Map of the walls of Dublin by Leonard R.
             Strangways, 1904".
Date:        1904
Maker:       Leonard R. (Ffleming) Strangways, antiquary and member of the RSAI,
             co-author with E. MacDowel Cosgrave of "The Dictionary of Dublin"
             (1895, 1908).
Publisher:   NOT CONFIRMED. The NLI Sources database lists a manuscript "Map
             of the walls of Dublin by Leonard R. Strangways, 1904" at Trinity
             College Library, Ms. 2931. The RSAI holds lantern slides of it
             (rsai.locloudhosting.net items 24974, 29144, 29145, 29146; that
             site was down for maintenance on 5 Nov 2026, so I couldn't check
             them). The Commons uploader's copy is a printed sheet "from some
             historical journal?". I found no printed imprint.
Death:       His obituary appeared in the Journal of the Royal Society of
             Antiquaries of Ireland, Series 7, Vol. III (1933), pp. 149-150,
             so he died around 1932-33 (exact date not confirmed). That is
             before 1956, so the map is public domain in Ireland (life + 70).
Downloaded:  https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/fa/Walls_of_Dublin_with_Bridge%2C_R_Poddle_and_other_waterways.jpg
File page:   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Walls_of_Dublin_with_Bridge,_R_Poddle_and_other_waterways.jpg
Licence:     Public domain ({{PD-old}} on Commons; "Own scan of PD work,
             freely reuseable").
Pixel size:  956 x 563. This is the only copy online. Commons has no larger
             version, and none turned up on archive.org. A ~2400 px image would
             need a new scan, e.g. from TCD Ms. 2931 or from the RSAI.
Credit line: Leonard R. Strangways, Map of the walls of Dublin, 1904. Wikimedia Commons (public domain).
