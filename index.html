Tallaght to Wellington Quay

The river that named Dublin

The Poddle is the most important watercourse in Dublin city, it provided its water, its defensive moat and powered its mills. It gave the city its name.

Poddle.ie follows it from source to sea, and tells the history of how monks, citizens and engineers harnessed it to meet a growing city’s needs.
