Classroom and family workbooks

Five workbooks about the Poddle, each written at three levels for 1st to 6th class, so fifteen in all. They are PDFs you download and print: original writing and artwork, with notes for the adult, curriculum links and an answer key. One copy covers your own family or a single classroom.

  • The Secret River

    A first look at Dublin’s hidden river: where it rises, where it runs and why the city grew beside it. Fact pages, drawing, matching and a map to colour.

    1st and 2nd class ages 6 to 83rd and 4th class ages 8 to 105th and 6th class ages 10 to 12

  • Hunt for the Hidden River

    A treasure hunt through the city centre, stop by stop, looking for the signs of a river that runs underneath. Clues, rubbings and an answer key.

    1st and 2nd class ages 6 to 83rd and 4th class ages 8 to 105th and 6th class ages 10 to 12

  • Walk the Hidden River

    A self-guided walking tour of the Poddle’s course through the Liberties and the city centre, about 2.5 km, with activities at each stop.

    1st and 2nd class ages 6 to 83rd and 4th class ages 8 to 105th and 6th class ages 10 to 12

  • Mill Detectives

    Water power and the mills of the Poddle, with a waterwheel you can make at home from card and a paper plate.

    1st and 2nd class ages 6 to 83rd and 4th class ages 8 to 105th and 6th class ages 10 to 12

  • Flood Defenders

    Rain, rivers and keeping Dublin dry: why the Poddle floods, what the flood scheme is building, and simple experiments to try.

    1st and 2nd class ages 6 to 83rd and 4th class ages 8 to 105th and 6th class ages 10 to 12
