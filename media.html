The Dame Street bank tunnel, 1985

Over the Easter weekend of 1985 a gang tunnelled from waste ground beside the Dublin Castle wall, crawled through the sewers under Dame Street and cut through a basement wall of the AIB branch. It was the ladies’ toilet. The alarm went off and they fled empty-handed. The news reports of the time don’t name the Poddle, but the river runs under this corner of Dame Street, and the film Dublin’s Hidden Rivers shows the wall in the culvert that the gang tried to use.
